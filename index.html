ABOUT US
Manhajul Hidaya is an Islamic institution affiliated with Bukhari Islamic Dawah College. The institution aims to produce Islamic scholars who can contribute positively to society. To achieve this goal, Manhajul Hidaya offers language studies in English, Arabic, Urdu, and other languages.By providing these language studies, the institution enables students to gain a deeper understanding of Islamic texts, communicate effectively with people from diverse backgrounds, and spread the message of Islam globally.
