ABOUT Fest

The "ALIF" Arts Festival is a captivating celebration of Islamic culture and creativity, showcasing the rich artistic heritage of the Muslim world. This vibrant festival features an array of exhibitions, performances, and workshops that highlight the beauty and diversity of Islamic art, including intricate calligraphy, stunning architecture, elegant textiles, and evocative poetry.
